Titan Arc

Industry:

Software

Location:

Spain

Our Role:

  • Visual Identity Design

The Challenge:

Titan Arc was a new venture entering the longevity and healthcare space with a bold vision but no defined brand. Starting with just a name, the goal was to build a premium identity system that could clearly articulate its positioning and support a growing ecosystem of sub-brands.

The Solution:

We developed a complete brand strategy and visual identity from the ground up, positioning Titan Arc as The Longevity Operating System. At the core is a modular identity system designed to unify its sub-brands - Oracle, Vault, Prophet, Angel, and Summit -under one cohesive framework. The result is a refined, scalable brand that communicates intelligence, precision, and a connected approach to modern healthcare.
