Client:

Numela

Industry:

Building and Construction

Location:

Ghana

Our Role:

  • Visual Identity Design

The Challenge:

Numela was a new venture entering the building and construction space with a bold vision but no defined brand. Starting with just a name, the goal was to build a premium identity system that could clearly articulate its positioning and support a growing ecosystem of sub-brands.

The Solution:

We developed a complete brand strategy and visual identity from the ground up, positioning Numela as The Building and Construction Operating System. At the core is a modular identity system designed to unify its sub-brands - Oracle, Vault, Prophet, Angel, and Summit -under one cohesive framework. The result is a refined, scalable brand that communicates intelligence, precision, and a connected approach to modern healthcare.
